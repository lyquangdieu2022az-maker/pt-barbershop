# PT Barbershop Luxury Website

Web gioi thieu thuong hieu PT Barbershop rieng cho khach xem.

## Mo tren may

Mo file `index.html` bang Chrome/Edge la xem duoc ngay.

## Dua len hosting

Day toan bo thu muc nay len hosting tinh nhu Hostinger, Netlify, Render Static Site hoac GitHub Pages.

Neu dung chung voi bo POS Render, ten mien goc se mo web gioi thieu, con `/pos/` se mo app order/POS.

## Can sua thong tin

- Doi hotline/dia chi: sua phan footer trong `index.html`. Mac dinh chi nhanh chinh: Xa Hau Nghia, Tay Ninh.
- Doi gia dich vu: sua cac the trong khu `Menu dich vu` va `Combo nang cap dien mao` cua `index.html`.
- Doi anh: thay file trong thu muc `assets`.
- Doi mau/bo cuc: sua `styles.css`.

## Luu y

Form dat lich hien tai tao phieu hen va cho sao chep de gui qua Zalo/Messenger.

Trang nay khong dung chung du lieu order/POS. Web gioi thieu la web public cho khach xem, con app POS la he thong noi bo de thu ngan in bill, ket ca va quan ly doanh thu. Trong trang co nut mo app order/POS de chuyen nhanh.

Neu muon khach dat lich tren web nay va lich tu chay vao app POS, can lam them backend/API dat lich chung voi POS.
